# 3D Asset List

## Environments
- Main riverfront festival world with water, shore, paths and plaza.
- Golden Temple landmark.
- Moon Bridge crossing.
- Lotus Garden.
- Starry Sky / lantern viewing area.
- Floating Market stalls.

## Characters
- Base avatar body variants.
- Hair, clothing, traditional-inspired outfits and accessories.
- Friendly cartoon festival characters and NPCs.
- Idle, walk, turn, wave, celebrate and photo poses.

## Krathong components
- Banana-leaf bases and tier variations.
- Lotus, marigold and jasmine decorations.
- Candle, incense and safe flame effects.
- Floating animation and water ripple effects.

## Materials and textures
- Water normals and reflection maps.
- Wood, stone, gold, fabric, leaves, flowers and painted surfaces.
- Building trim, roof and ornament atlases.

## Audio
- Ambient river, night insects and soft festival ambience.
- Original or properly licensed background music.
- UI feedback, footsteps, water and ceremony sound effects.

## Technical guidance
- Prefer optimized glTF/GLB models.
- Use texture atlases and compressed textures where supported.
- Create LODs for large environments and repeated props.
- Set budgets for polygon count, texture size and draw calls per target device.
- Keep asset source, license and attribution details in an asset register.
- Do not commit unlicensed third-party assets.
